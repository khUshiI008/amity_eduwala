FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions Before You Apply?

A quick overview of the programme and the next steps.

Are the programmes offered completely online?

The programmes featured on this page are online programmes. Check the current programme page and academic calendar for the latest delivery, examination and learner-support details.

What is the duration of the Online MBA?

The Online MBA is a two-year programme structured across four semesters.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Applicants generally need a recognised bachelor's degree. Exact eligibility, minimum marks and accepted qualifications should be confirmed against the current admission guidelines for the programme you select.

Can I choose an MBA specialisation?

Yes. The Online MBA offers multiple specialisation options. The currently available list may change, so confirm the active options with an authorised counsellor before applying.

Does the university provide placement support?

Career support may include profile building, employability assessments, interview preparation, recruiter access and placement-related events. Placement assistance is not a guarantee of a job or salary outcome.

How do the fee, EMI and cashback offer work?

Fees and payment plans can vary by admission cycle and offer eligibility. The two-EMI cashback promotion is subject to its current terms. Ask the admissions team to confirm the payable fee, EMI amount, cashback value and payout conditions in writing.
